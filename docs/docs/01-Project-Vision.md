# Project Vision

> **MentorAI – The AI That Learns How You Learn**

---

# Overview

MentorAI is an adaptive AI learning platform designed to transform the way students learn.

Instead of acting as a generic AI chatbot, MentorAI continuously learns about each student's knowledge, learning style, strengths, weaknesses, and progress. Using this information, it adapts its teaching strategy to maximise understanding and long-term retention.

The goal is not simply to answer questions but to become an intelligent learning companion that evolves alongside every student.

---

# Problem Statement

Students today learn from multiple disconnected resources:

- YouTube
- Documentation
- PDFs
- Online courses
- ChatGPT
- Coding platforms
- Personal notes

While these resources provide valuable information, they do not work together.

Current AI assistants generate answers but have little understanding of:

- What the student already knows
- Where the student struggles
- Which teaching style works best
- How learning has progressed over time

As a result, students often receive generic explanations that may not match their level of understanding.

---

# Vision Statement

Create an AI mentor that continuously adapts its teaching strategy based on how each individual student learns.

Every learner should receive explanations, quizzes, revision plans, and learning roadmaps that evolve with their progress.

---

# Mission

Empower students with an AI mentor that makes learning more personalised, engaging, and effective through adaptive teaching and continuous learning.

---

# Target Users

MentorAI is designed for:

- College students
- Self-learners
- Software engineering interview candidates
- Competitive programming learners
- Professionals learning new technologies

---

# Core Innovation

The foundation of MentorAI is the **Learning Twin**.

The Learning Twin is a continuously evolving representation of each student's learning journey.

It captures information such as:

- Knowledge level
- Weak topics
- Strong topics
- Preferred explanation style
- Learning speed
- Quiz performance
- Confidence level
- Learning history

Every AI interaction uses the Learning Twin to determine the most effective teaching strategy.

---

# Long-Term Goals

MentorAI aims to become an intelligent mentor capable of:

- Explaining concepts in multiple ways
- Detecting misconceptions
- Tracking long-term progress
- Generating personalised quizzes
- Creating adaptive learning roadmaps
- Providing interview preparation
- Producing revision notes automatically
- Supporting lifelong learning

---

# Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if it can:

- Improve student understanding through adaptive explanations
- Reduce repeated misconceptions
- Track measurable learning progress
- Deliver personalised learning experiences
- Maintain an evolving Learning Twin for every student

---

# Future Vision

MentorAI is not intended to replace teachers.

Its purpose is to provide every student with a personalised mentor that is always available, remembers previous learning sessions, and continuously adapts to help them achieve their goals.

---

# One-Sentence Vision

> **Build an AI mentor that learns how each student learns and teaches them in the most effective way possible.**
